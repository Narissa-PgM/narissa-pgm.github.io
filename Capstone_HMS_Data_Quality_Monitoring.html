What the numbers mean

Plain English for the key terms.
Flag: a warning the AI raises about a possible data problem.
Flag accuracy (precision): of the flags raised, the share that are real. 85% means 85 of 100.
Problems caught (recall): of all real problems, the share the AI finds.
Shadow mode: the AI flags, but nothing changes until we trust it.

All three tests must pass

Model quality without adoption delivers zero.

KPI framework: four families

Click a family for the measurement plan.
Plain-English glossary
False alarm: a flag that turns out not to be a problem.
Override: a steward disagrees with a flag and says why.
Drift (PSI): the data changes over time and the AI gets less accurate. PSI is the score; above 0.20 raises an alarm.
Tripwire: a measurable warning level that tells us a risk is happening.
Tier 1 / Tier 2: how critical a source is. Tier 1 is the most critical.
MTTR: mean time to repair. The target is 4 hours for pipeline problems.
