Five review domains — fairness, privacy, explainability, safety, accountability. Each item needs an owner and a piece of evidence, not just a tick.

One page · Pre-release gate
Owner: Ethics Board
A

Review Identification

B

Five Review Domains

Tick · assign owner · cite evidence
1Fairness
  • Protected attributes identified and documented
  • Disparate-impact testing completed on all subgroups
  • All four-fifths ratios ≥ 0.80 (or justified exception)
  • Proxy variables screened and excluded
  • Mitigation applied where disparity found
OwnerEvid.
2Privacy
  • Lawful basis / consent established and documented
  • Minimum-necessary data scope justified
  • De-identification or pseudonymization applied
  • Retention and deletion schedule defined
  • Access controls and audit logging verified
OwnerEvid.
3Explainability
  • Intended use and non-uses stated plainly
  • Top drivers available per decision
  • Explanation method validated for stability
  • Affected party can receive a plain-language reason
  • Limitations documented in the model card
OwnerEvid.
4Safety
  • Failure modes enumerated and ranked
  • Human-override path defined and reachable
  • Rollback tested within recovery target
  • Uncertainty threshold triggers fallback
  • Incident playbook reviewed with ops
OwnerEvid.
5Accountability
  • Named owner for every lifecycle stage
  • Decision rights and escalation path documented
  • Monitoring owner assigned post-release
  • Redress / appeal channel available to affected parties
  • Audit trail complete and immutable
