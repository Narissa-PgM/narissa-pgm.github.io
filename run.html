Rules of Use

The worksheet fails if these are broken
✓Do
  • Write what you heard, verbatim. "No time" and "doesn't fit my workflow" route to different fixes. Paraphrase destroys the diagnostic.
  • Route before you fix. Identify the deficient move and blocker category before deciding what to do. The order is the discipline.
  • Complete "Next Result" one cycle later. A fix without a measurement is a guess. The whole point of the log is seeing what worked.
  • Escalate when a blocker repeats 3×. Three appearances of the same category means the fix isn't touching the root cause.
  • Keep the log in one place. Two logs, two versions, two realities — the pattern gets lost.
✕Don't
  • Don't log a cycle without a survey. The rung has to come from measurement, not from memory of complaints.
  • Don't retrain when Ability is the broken rung. This is the single most common misdiagnosis. Ability failures are structural — fix the tool, the access, or the workflow.
  • Don't compress multiple fixes into one row. If three changes shipped, log three rows. Otherwise you can't tell which one moved the score.
  • Don't act on one low score. One cycle is a reading. Look for the trend and the concentration before spending money.
  • Don't leave "Next Result" blank. An unmeasured fix is a story, not a control.
